Focus

Research

Vaccine development, viral immunology, molecular evolution, and metagenomics— with emphasis on selected-agent pathogens of global agricultural importance.

  • Vaccine development

    Live-attenuated and inactivated strategies against African swine fever virus and highly pathogenic avian influenza.

  • Humoral immunity

    Profiling swine antibody responses to structural viral proteins under vaccination and natural infection.

  • Serological platforms

    Building and validating LIPS, LACA, and ELISA assays for sensitive detection of pathogen-specific antibodies.

  • Molecular virology

    Characterizing PRRSV, ASFV, and swine influenza viruses, including nanopore metagenomics and cell-line development.
