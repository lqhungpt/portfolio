Selected publications

Peer-reviewed work on Influenza vaccine, ASFV vaccine and diagnostics, and PRRSV.

  1. Lipid nanoparticle-encapsulated DNA vaccine prevented lung consolidation following heterologous influenza A virus challenge in pigs

    npj Vaccines, 2026 · Nguyen, Lai, Luong, et al. (co-author)

    Compared LNP-DNA and HA protein vaccines against a mismatched swine influenza A virus challenge. Neither prevented nasal shedding or induced cross-reactive HI antibodies, but the LNP-DNA vaccine blocked gross lung consolidation while the protein vaccine worsened lesions (VAERD), supporting LNP-DNA as a safer swine IAV vaccine platform.

  2. Evaluation of the diagnostic sensitivity and specificity of two pen-side tests for detecting African swine fever virus in experimentally infected pigs

    Archives of Virology, 2024 · Vu, Luong, et al. (co-first author)

    Compared a portable qPCR and a lateral flow antigen test for early ASFV detection in experimentally infected pigs. Portable qPCR matched lab reference testing and detected viral DNA in blood before clinical signs, while the antigen test was highly specific but less sensitive early and late after infection and performed poorly on oral swabs.

  3. Comparative analysis of swine antibody responses following vaccination with live-attenuated and killed African swine fever virus vaccines

    Vaccines, 2023 · Luong et al. (first author)

    Compared antibody profiles after live-attenuated versus killed ASFV vaccination and subsequent virulent challenge. Only live-attenuated vaccination protected all pigs and elicited strong responses to many structural antigens; reactivity to p12, p14, p15, p32, and pD205R correlated with reduced viremia and may mark vaccine-induced protection.

  4. Differential antibody responses in sows and finishing pigs naturally infected with African swine fever virus under field conditions

    Virus Research, 2022 · Luong et al. (first author)

    Profiled humoral responses in field survivors of natural ASFV infection. Nearly all animals responded to p12, p32, p54, and pp62, whereas antibodies to C-type lectin and CD2v were mainly found in sows, indicating age- or parity-linked differences in ASFV immunity.

  5. Evaluation of antibody response directed against porcine reproductive and respiratory syndrome virus structural proteins

    Vaccines, 2020 · Luong et al. (first author)

    Used a luciferase immunoprecipitation system (LIPS) to map antibody responses to all eight PRRSV structural proteins. Strongest reactivity targeted GP3, M, and N, with N-specific antibodies appearing earliest; N-based LIPS showed higher diagnostic sensitivity than GP3 on clinical sera.
